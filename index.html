How a PEG order goes

  1. Book a shop near you

    PEG lists the vetted laundry shops that serve your area. Pick one, choose the service, and book a pickup or a drop-off.

  2. The shop weighs your clothes

    You do not need to know the weight. The shop weighs the load at intake, and the app shows the weight and the fee.

  3. Pay in the app, then follow it home

    Pay in the app with MoMo or card. PEG tells you when your clothes are washing, ready and on their way back.

Pickup at your door, or drop-off at the shop

When the day is full, a rider collects the bag and brings it back folded. On your way past, drop it at the shop instead. Meet the PEG trike

Pay after the shop weighs your laundry

No guessing at the door. The fee is set by the weight on the shop's scale, and you see each line before you pay.

Questions about PEG

More on payments, pickup and shops in the full list. Read all the questions

When do I pay for my laundry?

After the shop confirms the weight. PEG then shows the fee, and you pay it in the app with Mobile Money or card through Paystack.

Do I need to know the weight of my laundry?

No. Laundry is priced by weight, so the shop weighs the load at intake before anyone quotes a fee.

Does the PEG app keep a wallet?

No. PEG holds no money for you and moves none between users. Cards and MoMo stay with Paystack.

When can I download the PEG app?

PEG is coming to the App Store and Google Play. Join the waitlist and we email you when it is out.
