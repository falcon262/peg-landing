# PEG home page plan (tasteskill 2.5.1)

1. Kind: software (a consumer laundry pickup app for Accra, with PEG Partner for shops). Page type software, site-kind software.
2. Deal:
   - hero `collage`: a `.device` phone holding PEG's live order screen (picked up, weighed at 4.2 kg, GH₵ 42.00 to pay) on the sage lit ground, and the weigh-in card looping through its three steps
   - palette: PEG's own brand through `start.mjs brand` (bg #f8f8f8, surface #ffffff, ink #131313, PEG yellow #f2cc44 from the splash screen and the app's buttons); type `mona`, radius soft
   - lit ground `sage` (dealt), which sits beside the yellow and PEG Partner's green
   - moments `pinned-chapters` (#how: the three steps of an order, each with the app's own screen) and `liquid-hover` (#door: the rider at the door). `line-carries-colour` and `iris-opens` were dealt first and re-dealt: they need a section on a new ground, and PEG's surface (#ffffff) is too close to its page (#f8f8f8) to show one
   - nav `nav-centre`, footer `footer-dark`, close `close-word` ("Peg it!", the app's own call from its splash screen), rhythm `surface-pairs` (#services and #partner), buttons `solid`; the mark is PEG's logo

## Fact sheet

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| What it is | A laundry pickup app for Accra, run by Falcon Solutions |
| How it works | Book a vetted shop that serves your area; a rider collects, or you drop off; the shop weighs the load at intake; you pay in the app; push updates at each step |
| Services | Wash & Fold, Dry Clean, Iron Only, Bedding |
| Price | By weight (and per item), set at the shop's weigh-in, itemised on the order |
| Payment | Mobile Money or card through Paystack, after the weigh-in; PEG keeps no wallet |
| Shops | PEG Partner app; apply with shop details and a Ghana Card; Falcon approves; payouts to MoMo or bank |
| Availability | Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play; waitlist by email to info@falconsolutions.io |
| Material | The app's own designs (FreshPress/docs), PEG's photographs (public/assets/client, partner) |

Voice: warm-plain. "You" for the customer, "PEG" for the app. Sentences 8 to 18 words. Never: invented prices, delivery promises or shop counts.

- `how` (2): pinned-chapters, three steps with the app's feed, tracking and notifications screens.

- `pay` (1): pay after the weigh-in; a live receipt on the lit ground.

- `partner` (1): PEG Partner for shops; the shop photograph.

- `waitlist` (1): the form (name, neighbourhood, email) beside PEG's wordmark on its yellow.
- `footer.closing`: close-word "Peg it!" and footer-dark.
